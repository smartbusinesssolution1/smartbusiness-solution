Attendance Face Match — Test Page

Upload a stored profile photo, then use the webcam — capture is automatic: the oval turns green once your face is correctly centered, sized, and facing forward, and a blink while it's green triggers the burst capture (camera turns off right after). A blink from far away, off-center, or at an angle will not capture — the oval must be green first. This exercises POST /api/v1/attendance/mark-burst (recommended) or falls back to POST /api/v1/attendance/mark for plain photo uploads (no blink check possible on a single still photo).
