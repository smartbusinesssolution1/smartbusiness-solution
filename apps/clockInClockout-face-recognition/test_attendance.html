Attendance Face Match — Test Page

Live camera only. Upload the stored profile photo, start the camera and put your face inside the oval — it turns green only when your whole face is inside (red otherwise). Hold still a moment, then blink to capture. Capture is automatic and the camera turns off. Uses GET /api/v1/attendance/challenge + POST /api/v1/attendance/mark-burst. (Random head turns are available via CHALLENGE_STEPS on the server but off by default.)
